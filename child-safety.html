QuillTalk — Child Safety Standards

QuillTalk is designed for users aged 13 and older. We take child safety seriously and follow Google Play’s Families Policy and general content safety standards.

Age Restriction

Our app does not target or market to children under 13. Access to QuillTalk is intended only for teens and adults aged 13 and above.

Content & Interactions

All user-generated content and interactions within QuillTalk must follow our community guidelines. Any inappropriate, harmful, or unsafe content may result in account moderation or removal.

Safety Measures

We monitor and review reports of harmful or abusive behavior. We also provide tools for users to report violations to help maintain a safe and respectful environment.

Parental Concerns

If a parent or guardian believes their child under 13 has created an account, they may contact us at support@quilltalk.org for immediate assistance.
