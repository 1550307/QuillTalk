Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2025

QuillTalk (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the QuillTalk app (“Service”). This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Service.

By using QuillTalk, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you sign up for QuillTalk:

  • Username — used to identify your account.
  • Email Address — used for account verification and important notifications.

We also collect and store chat messages exchanged between users to allow message history and chat functionality within the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Send verification codes and account-related emails.
  • Enable chat features and peer-to-peer communication.
  • Improve app performance and maintain security.

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your account data and messages are stored securely in our database (managed through PHPMyAdmin). We take reasonable precautions to protect your data, but no online system is completely secure.

4. Third-Party Services

Currently, QuillTalk does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or external hosting services. If this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy to reflect those services.

5. Your Rights

You can:

  • Access or update your account details.
  • Request deletion of your account or data.
  • Choose to stop receiving emails or notifications.

To do so, contact us using the details below.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When updates are made, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
