Account Deletion Information

This page provides details on how to permanently delete your QuillTalk account.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Log in to the **QuillTalk app** or website.
  2. Go to the **Settings** or **Profile** section.
  3. Tap or click the **"Delete My Account"** button (it is usually red).
  4. Confirm the irreversible action when prompted.

What Data is Deleted?

When you delete your account, **all associated data** is permanently erased from our system:

  • Your User Profile (username, email, password hash).
  • All Sent and Received Messages.
  • All Friend Relationship records.
  • All Active Session Tokens (you will be logged out everywhere).

Request Deletion via Email (Alternative)

If you cannot access the app, you may request deletion by email. Please include your **QuillTalk Username** and **Email Address** linked to the account.
